# INVEST OS

INVEST OS is an investment workspace for a fund team: discover companies, collect evidence, challenge a thesis, record a human decision, and monitor its consequences.

The user brief asks for extreme simplicity, clear next actions, trustworthy evidence, and a complete investment-team perspective. The current delivery is a local sample-data application. The user initially requested production and subsequently requested a complete sample dataset; real authentication and integrations remain explicit production requirements, not completed features.

## Users

- Managing partner: thesis, oversight and decisions.
- Analyst: discovery, research, diligence and follow-ups.
- Fund operations: commitments, capital records and reporting.
- Limited partner: reports and portfolio information.
- Founder: pitch submission.

## Product truth

Companies and financials are illustrative. The role picker is not security. AI provider configuration does not prove successful connectivity. AI outputs are drafts; external source verification is not connected. Local records persist in a JSON store; pipeline decisions and strategy persist in browser storage. No funds move and no email is sent.

## Core journey

Login → Today → Discover → Company profile → Deal workspace / Diligence → Committee → Decision history / Memo → Portfolio → Follow-up task.

## Success criteria

A new user identifies the next action without instruction; a user can distinguish sample material from verified evidence; tasks and research survive reload; decision rationale is findable; primary workflows remain usable on desktop, tablet and mobile.
