# Navigation simplification

The primary menu now has five task areas instead of nineteen competing destinations.

| Area | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Today | Briefing and personal tasks |
| Discover | Companies, market signals, research |
| Deals | Pipeline, reviews, history, submissions |
| Portfolio | Investments and performance |
| Fund | Overview, commitments, reports, contacts |

Settings, investment strategy, integrations and help live in a separate utility area. Role permissions continue to filter destinations.

Board and List are views of the same pipeline. Company pages share Overview, Evidence, Analysis, Decision and Memo navigation. Evidence groups its eight capture types into Summary, Pitch, Evidence and Team. Final verdicts are recorded only in Decision; individual team votes remain in Team. Signal source cards are collapsed so the feed leads the page.

Validate with representative users: finding a company, reviewing evidence, recording a decision, and finding a fund report. Measure completion, wrong turns and time on task; an award or a 10/10 score is not established by implementation alone.
